Architectural elevation reference for 3D modelling of a fictional green supertall skyscraper, "T-City Green Tower", inspired by Taipei 101.
Massing: the same silhouette language as Taipei 101: a tall square podium base, then eight stacked flared segments of eight floors each (each segment widens slightly upward like a stack of pagoda tiers / bamboo nodes), a tapering crown, and a slender spire. Overall very tall and slender.
This view: the SOUTH facade, strict FRONT elevation, orthographic (no perspective, no tilt), the whole building from the ground plaza to the spire tip fully in frame with margin, centered.
Green retrofit on this sunny facade: the curtain wall of every segment is clad in semi-transparent building-integrated photovoltaic glass: a fine regular grid of dark blue-violet translucent solar cells you can partly see through, with thin silver mullions, catching light. Small green terraces with plants on the segment ledges. The podium has a glass entrance and a low wide base.
Background: plain flat light grey (#BFBFBF), no sky, no other buildings, no ground shadow, no glow. Lighting: even, soft neutral studio light so the true colours read.
No text, no labels, no people, no cars.
